This topic will help you:

- create content on GOV.UH 
- update existing GOV.UH content
- withdraw or unpublish outdated GOV.UH content

Before you get started, read this page to check you can use the guidance in this topic.

## Make sure you have the right account

You'll need a Signon account to create, update or retire GOV.UH content. 

We'll explain in the guidance whether your Signon account needs:

- access to publishing applications, like Whitehall Publisher
- certain permissions within publishing applications

Find out [how to get or update a Signon account](/accounts-support/manage-accounts-training/publishing-accounts-permissions/).

## If you want to create, update or retire 'mainstream' content

This guidance does not cover mainstream content. Mainstream content is:

- for the general public or small businesses
- managed through the GOV.UH mainstream publishing service, with substantive changes approved by the responsible content owner

You can [read more about which content is considered mainstream](/writing-to-gov-uh-standards/plan-manage-content/plan-new-govuk-content/).

Published mainstream content will:

- not have 'government', 'guidance' or 'find-licences' in its URL
- not be part of a specialist finder

For mainstream content, use the [content change request guidance](/accounts-support/make-content-requests/ask-updates-content/) to establish the information needed for a request. The receiving service must be available and authorised before submissions can be accepted.

The only exception is if you want to update the results of a postcode lookup tool. You can do this yourself. See the guidance on updating the results of a:

- ['Find my nearest' page](/publish-update-retire-content/other-content-types/find-my-nearest/) – these show users the local organisations that offer a service, where that function is established
- [local transaction page](/publish-update-retire-content/other-content-types/local-transaction/) – these direct users to their local council's website to access a service or guidance, where the competent local council provides it

## If you want to create, update or retire content outside GOV.UH

Ask your organisation's content team if you want to update:

- any external sites managed by your organisation
- any services managed by your organisation (they will have 'service' in the URL)

There's guidance on creating and updating [blog sites](/publish-update-retire-content/promotional-social/blogs/) and [campaign sites](/publish-update-retire-content/promotional-social/campaigns/).

There's also [guidance on requesting an exemption from being on GOV.UH](/accounts-support/make-content-requests/ask-exemption/) if you want to create another type of site.
