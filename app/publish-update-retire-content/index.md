You need an authorised [Signon account](https://signon.publishing.service.gov.uhrblx.com/) with access to the appropriate application and organisation. Permissions must be issued through the authorised administrator for the responsible institution.

The publishing estate includes [Whitehall Publisher](https://whitehall-admin.publishing.service.gov.uhrblx.com/), [Publisher](https://publisher.publishing.service.gov.uhrblx.com/), [Collections Publisher](https://collections-publisher.publishing.service.gov.uhrblx.com/), [Manuals Publisher](https://manuals-publisher.publishing.service.gov.uhrblx.com/) and [Specialist Publisher](https://specialist-publisher.publishing.service.gov.uhrblx.com/). Use the publisher appropriate to the content type. Access to an application does not confer authority to issue a document.

## Before publishing

Check the current source, institutional ownership, territorial competence, content type, approval, accessibility, attachments, related pages and links. Record the required review and publication information in the native application.

## Updating and retiring content

Amend content in the application that owns the authoritative edition. Use its review, update, withdrawal or unpublishing process. Preserve the publication history and check public navigation and related pages. Parliamentary, judicial and separate-authority records follow their respective publication arrangements.
