The United Hampshire Government publishes this content and publishing guidance. The [GOV.UH privacy notice](https://www.gov.uhrblx.com/help/privacy-notice/) provides information about the government's website, the use of personal information and how to contact the responsible service.

## Visiting this guidance

The guidance consists of published information pages. Reading a page does not submit a service application or create a publishing account. Website hosting and security systems may process information about requests to deliver the site and protect it against misuse.

This guidance does not use an analytics or mailing-list integration. See the [cookie information](/cookies/) for the cookies used by the guidance site.

## Using publishing applications

Signon and the individual publishing applications maintain access and audit records for their own services. Refer to their applicable privacy information when accessing those systems.
