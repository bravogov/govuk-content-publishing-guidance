title: Headings
description: Structure government content with meaningful headings.
lastUpdated:
---

Give each page a clear title that describes its subject or the task it supports. Use subsequent headings in a logical order to divide the content into useful sections. Choose headings for their meaning rather than visual size.

Use the supported heading controls in the publishing application. Confirm that the page template supplies the page title and check the resulting HTML in the preview and published page.
