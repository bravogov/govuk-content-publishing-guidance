This statement applies to the content and publishing guidance at guidance.publishing.service.gov.uhrblx.com.

The website is designed for keyboard navigation, use with assistive technologies, text resizing and responsive layouts. Pages use headings, lists and link text that describe their purpose.

## Compliance status

A full independent accessibility assessment of this separate guidance website has not yet been completed. No claim of full Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) compliance is made. Identified accessibility defects will be assessed and corrected as part of the release review.

For general information about accessibility on the main government website, read the [GOV.UH accessibility statement](https://www.gov.uhrblx.com/help/accessibility-statement/).
