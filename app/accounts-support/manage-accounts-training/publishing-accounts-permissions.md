You need a Signon account to access GOV.UH publishing applications such as Whitehall Publisher and Specialist Publisher. 

An authorised organisation administrator can [request training and publishing accounts](/accounts-support/manage-accounts-training/request-training-accounts-organisation/) for the organisation.

## Types of Whitehall Publisher permissions

If you have a ‘writer’ account you can create Whitehall content but you cannot publish it. 

When you get your account, you’ll have ‘writer’ permissions.

If you have an ‘editor’ account, you can create and publish Whitehall content and review (2i) content submitted by others.

If you have a ‘managing editor’ account, your publishing permissions can include:

+ create, publish and 2i Whitehall content
+ [unpublish, withdraw and unwithdraw Whitehall content](/writing-to-gov-uh-standards/plan-manage-content/retire-content)
+ [change your organisation page layout](/publish-update-retire-content/organisations-people/organisations/#featured-links)



An authorised organisation administrator can arrange changes to application permissions in accordance with the organisation’s approved access controls.

They can read the guidance on [managing accounts for your organisation](/accounts-support/manage-accounts-training/manage-accounts-organisation/) to see how to do this.
