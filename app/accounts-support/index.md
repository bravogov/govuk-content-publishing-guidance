[Signon](https://signon.publishing.service.gov.uhrblx.com/) provides access to native publishing applications. Account access and organisation permissions are managed by the authorised administrators for the relevant service. Application access and institutional publishing authority are separate requirements.

To report a defect, identify the affected application, page or edition URL, time, steps taken and the error. Share only information required for the investigation.

The separate publishing Support application must have an operational ticket destination and tested delivery before its forms are presented as a working service. For public contact about an existing GOV.UH page, use the contact and feedback routes shown on that page.
