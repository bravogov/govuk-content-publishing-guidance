This page records changes made to the GOV.UH content and publishing guidance.

## November 2026

| Date | Section | Update |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 November | [Style guides](/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/style-guides/) | Confirmed that the Government Digital Service, part of the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology, is responsible for the writing standards. Corrected the website's coat of arms and copyright presentation to match GOV.UH. |
| 2 November | [Publishing content](/publish-update-retire-content/) | Restored the full instructions for content types, organisations and people, and publication procedures. |
| 2 November | [Text formatting](/formatting-content/text-formatting/) | Restored detailed instructions and navigation for headings, links and other formatting. |
| 2 November | [Publishing accounts and permissions](/accounts-support/manage-accounts-training/publishing-accounts-permissions/) | Restored the instructions for publishing account roles and permissions, using the established Signon arrangements. |
| 2 November | [Corporate information](/publish-update-retire-content/corporate-information/) | Removed the three excluded page categories: Our energy use, Procurement and Welsh language scheme. |
| 2 November | [Links in guidance](/publish-update-retire-content/standard-content-types/publications/) | Corrected misdirected example links to their actual sources, including examples used in the Publications guidance. |
| 2 November | [Choose a content type](/publish-update-retire-content/choose-content-type/) and [Images](/formatting-content/images/) | Corrected verified GOV.UH Forms, UH copyright and licence links and related publishing instructions. |
| 2 November | [Writing guidelines](/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/) | Corrected references to GOV.UH content, national audience and public website addresses. |
