This guidance explains how government editors prepare, review, publish, maintain and retire content on GOV.UH. It covers writing, choosing the correct publishing application and content type, preparing attachments, and managing published information.

Use it alongside the United Hampshire Government Drafting, Document, Legislation and Repository Standard, the instructions for your authorised publishing application, and your institution's publication arrangements. Content and editorial guidance does not confer permission to publish for another institution.

Government material is issued by its responsible government publisher. Parliamentary, judicial, devolved and local authority records remain the responsibility of their own institutions and publication services.

This guidance is maintained as a version-controlled publication. Substantive publishing requirements are reviewed against the applications and current government standards before release.
